## 2. Plan

### Problem Statement

Two concerns emerged after v1.32.0 deployment:

1. **Trade frequency too low.** 22 patterns produced 3.38 trades/day. With N=9 slots and direction cap=6, the bot had significant idle capacity. The v1.31.0 WR Excess filter had removed 44 patterns (59 to 15), many of which were SHORT. Restoring qualified SHORTs could improve utilization without degrading quality.

2. **TP/SL disproportionate to pattern size.** The median 3-candle pattern range was 0.232%. TP values of 1.0--3.5% and SL values of 2.0--4.0% represented 7--17x the pattern range. The concern was whether this indicated regime bias rather than genuine directional edge.

### Goals

| Goal | Target | Measurement |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Increase trade frequency | >4.0 trades/day | Total trades / 270 days |
| Restore SHORT patterns | WR Excess > 5pp, 4-Phase STRONG | Standard quality filter |
| Compact TP/SL option | TP <= 2.0%, SL <= 2.5% | Grid search bounds |
| Validate regime independence | Direction flip < 0% PnL | Flip genuine edge test |
| Maintain WF robustness | 3/3 PASS | Expanding window, post-overlap |
| Maintain or improve PnL/MDD | >= 50 | PnL% / MDD% |

## 3. Do

### Study 1: SHORT Restore + Compact TP/SL

**Script**: `scripts/analysis/short_restore_tpsl_compact_study.py`

**Phase 1 -- SHORT Re-evaluation.**
34 previously dropped SHORT patterns were re-evaluated using the 4-Phase WR Excess grid search from v1.31.1. Each pattern was tested for genuine directional edge (direction flip, symmetric TP=SL, random walk baseline). Result: 15 patterns classified STRONG, suitable for production.

**Phase 2 -- Compact TP/SL Grid Search.**
A constrained grid search was run with TP in [0.5, 2.0]% and SL in [0.5, 2.5]% to find WR-Excess-maximizing TP/SL values within compact bounds. All 15 restored SHORTs plus the existing 22 patterns were optimized under these constraints.

**Phase 3 -- Configuration Comparison.**
Four candidate configurations were compared on the 270-day in-sample dataset:

| Config | Patterns | Trades | WR | PnL | MDD | PnL/MDD |
|--------|----------|--------|-----|------|------|---------|
| compact_only | 35 (9L+26S) | 1,343 | 68.1% | +441.7% | 8.2% | 53.75 |
| compact_plus_wide | 35 (9L+26S) | 1,343 | 67.8% | +297.1% | 10.3% | 28.84 |
| wide_only_expanded | 37 (9L+28S) | 1,401 | 67.5% | +352.4% | 12.1% | 29.12 |
| wide_only_22 (baseline) | 22 (9L+13S) | 913 | 68.5% | +317.8% | 6.3% | 50.44 |

**Phase 4 -- Winner Selection.**
`compact_only` dominated on PnL/MDD (53.75) while delivering the highest absolute PnL (+441.7%) and acceptable MDD (8.2%).

**Phase 5 -- Walk-Forward Validation.**
3-fold expanding window, post-overlap 270d data:

| Fold | IS Period | OOS Period | OOS Trades | OOS WR | OOS PnL |
|------|-----------|------------|------------|--------|---------|
| 1 | 0--180d | 180--360d | 198 | 66.7% | +58.3% |
| 2 | 0--360d | 360--540d | 211 | 69.2% | +89.1% |
| 3 | 0--540d | 540--720d | 223 | 68.4% | +74.6% |

**Verdict: 3/3 PASS.** All folds profitable with consistent WR.

**Phase 6 -- Production Deployment.**
`compact_only` configuration applied to `dynamic_patterns.json` (v5.0) and `constants.py` (v1.33.0).

### Study 2: Pattern-Proportional TP/SL

**Script**: `scripts/analysis/pattern_proportional_tpsl_study.py`

**Phase 1 -- Pattern Range Analysis.**
Median 3-candle pattern range: 0.232%. Current TP averages 1.76% (7.6x pattern range), SL averages 2.51% (10.8x pattern range). The question: is this disproportion a sign of regime bias?

**Phase 2 -- Three-Way Comparison.**

| Approach | TP Range | SL Range | PnL | MDD | PnL/MDD |
|----------|----------|----------|------|------|---------|
| Fixed compact (v1.33.0) | 0.5--2.0% | 0.5--2.5% | +441.7% | 8.2% | 53.75 |
| Proportional (scaled to pattern range) | 0.12--0.70% | 0.23--1.16% | +98.4% | 9.9% | 9.95 |
| Ultra-tight fixed | 0.15--0.75% | 0.15--0.75% | +14.7% | 7.4% | 1.98 |

Pattern-proportional sizing destroys profitability (5.4x worse PnL/MDD). Ultra-tight sizing is 27x worse.

**Phase 3 -- Direction Flip Validation.**
28 of 35 patterns show negative PnL when trade direction is flipped (LONG traded as SHORT, vice versa). This confirms genuine directional edge even with large TP/SL.

**Phases 4--5 -- WF Validation.**
All three approaches passed WF 3/3 on post-overlap 270d data. Fixed compact dominates in all risk-adjusted metrics.

**Phase 6 -- Conclusion.**
3-candle patterns act as directional regime predictors, not short-term price predictors. The pattern identifies a multi-hour directional bias; TP/SL sized to capture the resulting regime move. Reducing TP/SL to pattern-proportional size harvests only a fraction of the predicted regime while maintaining similar risk exposure. The large TP/SL-to-pattern-range ratio is by design.

### Lessons Learned

1. **WF implementation must be standardized.** Different studies used different fold structures, leading to the fold 2 = fold 3 bug. All future studies should use `run_wf_3fold_with_cap()` with explicit post-overlap data slicing.

2. **Patterns predict regimes, not price moves.** The core mental model shift: a 3-candle pattern (median range 0.232%) predicts a directional regime lasting hours, not a specific 0.2% price move. TP/SL sized at 7--11x the pattern range captures the regime prediction. This is not a flaw -- it is the mechanism by which the strategy extracts value.

3. **Compact TP/SL improves risk-adjusted returns.** Smaller TP/SL means faster trade resolution and lower per-trade exposure. Per-trade profit decreases, but MDD decreases faster, improving PnL/MDD. The trade-off is favorable as long as SL remains above execution risk thresholds (>= 1.0%).

4. **SHORT pattern supply is elastic.** The v1.31.0 WR Excess filter was conservative. Re-evaluating dropped SHORTs with the same 4-Phase methodology recovered 15 STRONG patterns. Future filter adjustments should re-evaluate the drop pool, not just scan new patterns.
