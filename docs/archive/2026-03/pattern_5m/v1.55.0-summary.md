## The Big Discovery

### What We Learned

Through 4 critical research studies (rr_holdtime, rr_random_discrimination, direction_regime, data_contamination), we discovered:

**Mechanism stack generates 86% of PnL, not pattern prediction:**
```
PnL Sources:
├─ Cascade SL Tightening: 34%
├─ Agg Risk Cap: 28%
├─ Direction Cap: 15%
├─ Momentum Guard: 5%
└─ Pattern Edge: 14% ← ONLY 14%!
```

**WF validation is non-discriminating:**
- All 131 real patterns: WF 3/3 PASS (100%)
- All 30 random signals: WF 3/3 PASS (100%)
- Conclusion: WF cannot distinguish signal from noise; mechanism stack is primary edge

**SHORT direction structurally weak in bull regimes:**
- Bull regime: LONG +82% WR vs SHORT +62% WR (-20pp gap)
- This gap is architectural (mean reversion to bullish, vol clustering)
- Cannot be fixed at entry level; requires regime filter (not yet implemented)

### Why This Matters

The strategy is **not** a pattern recognition system. It's a **position management system**:
- Patterns provide discovery criterion only (MAE/MFE scan finds entries)
- Guards provide the edge (cascade SL stops loss amplification, agg risk limits correlated losses)
- WR expectation should be ~65% (with variance), not 90%+

This reframing prevents overconfidence and sets realistic performance targets.
